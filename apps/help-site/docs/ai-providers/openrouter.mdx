[OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/) is the AI service Toby uses. One account
gives Toby access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral,
DeepSeek, xAI, and more.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → Account** and click **Sign in with OpenRouter**.
2. Sign in or create an account in your browser, then approve Toby.
3. Return to Toby. The key OpenRouter issues is stored in your Mac's
   **Keychain**. It never leaves your Mac except to talk to OpenRouter.

The approval page may show Toby as a `localhost` address: that's a temporary
listener on your Mac that receives the sign-in. Click **Cancel** in Toby to stop
waiting. To revoke the key, use your [OpenRouter keys page](https://openrouter.ai/keys);
**Sign Out** in Toby removes it from this Mac.

## Credits

**Settings → Account** shows the credits left on your account. Use **Add
Credits…** to top up. If the account runs out, Toby shows a notice with a link
back to Account; add credits and try again.

## Models

Toby uses tuned defaults for each kind of work. To pick a different model for
chats, choose **Settings → Account → Advanced → Chat model** (the list comes
from OpenRouter's live catalog and shows models that support tools), or set a
model on a [persona](../personas).

## Web search

When Toby needs current information it searches the web through OpenRouter.
There is nothing to set up.
