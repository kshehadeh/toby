# Recordings

[Recordings](../listen) are transcribed **on your Mac** with Apple speech
recognition. Nothing is uploaded for transcription. After a recording is
transcribed, Toby writes a summary and, when the recording has no name yet, a
short title (these use your [OpenRouter](../ai-providers/openrouter) account).

Open **Settings → Recordings**.

| Setting | Purpose |
| ------- | ------- |
| **Record microphone** / **Record system audio** | Which sources a recording captures (both on by default; at least one stays on) |
| **Delete audio after transcription** | Remove a recording's audio once its transcript is saved (default on). The transcript, summary, and recording entry are kept. Keep audio if you want to transcribe again later. |
| **Summary persona** | The [persona](../personas) that writes recording summaries (defaults to your default persona) |

## When transcription runs

- While recording, Toby transcribes live and keeps the transcript when you stop.
- If a live transcript isn't complete, Toby transcribes the saved audio after you stop.
- **Transcribe** in the Recordings window runs it again for a recording whose audio was kept.

## If transcription fails

1. Check **Settings → Permissions** for **Speech Recognition** and **Microphone**.
2. Open **Recordings**, select the recording, and transcribe again (needs the audio).
3. A recording with no speech is kept with its audio so you can retry.
