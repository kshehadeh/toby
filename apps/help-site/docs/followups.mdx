# Automatic follow-ups

Toby can turn connected activity into [Toby Todos](./todos) in the **From Toby**
list. It tracks:

- **Replies you owe**, such as a direct question that still needs an answer.
- **Replies you're waiting for**, after a waiting period (three days by default).
- **Commitments you made**, such as promising to send a deck.

A model checks the evidence before Toby adds or resolves a follow-up. These are
todos, not permission for Toby to send mail or carry out the task.

## Set up follow-ups

1. Connect an [email account](./integrations/email) or another supported activity source.
2. Open **Settings → Follow-ups**.
3. Leave **Evaluator** on **Automatic**, or choose a configured model that
   supports structured output. Automatic tries Toby's auxiliary model, then
   the model used by The Feed.
4. Choose sources and kinds, then select **Save Changes**.
5. Select **Check now** to run an immediate check.

Email, calendar, and usable memories are on by default. Toby chats and todos
provide evidence for updating existing follow-ups. Connected Slack activity
can also contribute. Private and confirmation-required memories stay out of
follow-up context. Source status shows the last check or a readable error.
Forgetting a memory or making it private removes its stored activity copy too.

If no eligible evaluator is configured, Check now reports what needs setup
instead of adding todos without a model judgment.

## See why Toby added a todo

Open **Todos**, filter to the **From Toby** list, and select an item.
**Why Toby added this** shows its kind, confidence, counterpart when known,
and source evidence with reasons and links. You can also ask in chat,
“Why did Toby add this follow-up?”

Toby can complete the todo when new evidence shows the work is done, such as
a sent reply or a related chat update. A vague intention is not completion.
Enable completion notifications for a quiet summary.

### Undo or give feedback

- Choose **Reopen** on a completed follow-up to undo it. Toby needs evidence
  from after you reopened it before completing it again.
- Choose **Not useful** and confirm to delete the todo and record feedback.
- You can complete or delete the todo yourself using the usual todo controls.

Old evidence may no longer be available after retention cleanup. Inactive
follow-up tracking expires after 30 days; that does not delete the todo.

## Tune the checks

Settings → Follow-ups lets you change the waiting period, confidence
thresholds, check interval, and daily evaluation limit. Higher thresholds
require stronger evidence. Automatic checks default to every 30 minutes,
during The Feed's active hours, while the background service runs.

Turn off automatic completion if you want to keep completing todos yourself.
Toby can still mark its tracking loop resolved while leaving your todo open.
Turn off **Create follow-up todos** to stop automatic checks.

Add instructions such as “Prioritize direct client requests” or
“Ignore routine status updates.” Instructions save separately from settings.
You can keep up to 20, each up to 2,000 characters.

Follow-ups reuse [memories](./memories) for context, but do not automatically
save new memories from email or other activity. Follow-up tracking stays on
this Mac; the todos use normal [todo sync](./todos#sync-across-macs-and-backups).
