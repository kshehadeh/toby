# Connect your apps

Toby becomes useful when it can see your email, calendar, and tasks. These are
called **sources**. Connect only the ones you want; you can add more later.

| If you use… | Connect | Toby can then… |
| ----------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Apple Calendar (including Google or Exchange calendars added to it) | [Apple Calendar](../integrations/apple-calendar) | Show your day, find free time, create and move events |
| Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, or another mail account | [Email](../integrations/email) | Summarize unread mail, search, draft and send replies |
| Apple Reminders | [Apple Reminders](../integrations/apple-reminders) | List what's due, add tasks, mark them done |
| Slack | [Slack](../integrations/slack) | Catch your mentions, search, send messages, and reply to DMs |

Toby also has its own [todos](../todos), which need no setup.

## Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and macOS

These need no password. Toby asks macOS for permission the first time it uses
them. Review or change access under **Settings → Permissions**.

## Email and Slack

1. Open **Settings** (the gear in the toolbar, or **⌘,**) and choose **Sources**.
2. Click **Connect…** next to **Mail** or **Slack** and follow the steps.
3. The row shows the connected address or workspace. **Disconnect** removes the
   credentials from your Keychain.
