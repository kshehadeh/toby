# Set up your AI

Toby uses [OpenRouter](../ai-providers/openrouter) to understand your requests
and write answers. OpenRouter is one account that reaches models from OpenAI,
Anthropic, Google, and others. You sign in from your browser, so there's no key
to copy and paste.

1. Open **Settings → Account** (or click **Connect** next to **Configure AI
   provider** in the Home checklist).
2. Click **Sign in with OpenRouter**.
3. Your browser opens. Sign in or create an OpenRouter account, then approve
   access for Toby.
4. Return to Toby. The Account pane shows **Signed in** and your remaining
   credits.

When you're signed in, try asking: "Help me plan my day."

<Callout type="tip" title="What does it cost?">

You pay OpenRouter for what you use, usually a fraction of a cent per message.
Add a few dollars of credit to start (**Settings → Account → Add Credits…**).
Toby tells you if your account runs out of credit.

</Callout>

## Change the model (optional)

Toby picks tuned models for each job: a capable model for chats, and fast or
structured models for background work like Home, follow-ups, and titles.

- **Settings → Account → Advanced → Chat model** overrides the model for every
  chat.
- Each [persona](../personas) can also choose its own model.

Most people never need to change this.
