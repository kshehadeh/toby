Connect Toby to any IMAP/SMTP mailbox to read, search, and organize email from chat.

Email is built into Toby; there is nothing to install.

## Prerequisites

- An email account that supports **IMAP** (for reading mail) and **SMTP** (for sending mail)
- Your IMAP **host**, **port**, **username**, and **password**
- Your SMTP **host**, **port**, **username**, and **password**

<Callout type="tip" title="Guided setup">

Open **Settings → Sources** and click **Connect…** next to **Mail**. Enter your email address and Toby looks up the IMAP and SMTP servers for that domain. Confirm the servers, add your password (often an app password), and connect.

</Callout>

## How IMAP/SMTP credentials work

Toby uses standard IMAP and SMTP credentials instead of OAuth. You provide:

- **IMAP host** and **port** (e.g. `imap.gmail.com:993` for Gmail, `imap.fastmail.com:993` for Fastmail, `outlook.office365.com:993` for Outlook/Microsoft 365)
- **IMAP username** (usually your email address) and **password** (use an **App Password** if your provider requires one — see below)
- **SMTP host** and **port** (e.g. `smtp.gmail.com:465` for Gmail, `smtp.fastmail.com:465` for Fastmail, `smtp.office365.com:587` for Outlook/Microsoft 365)
- **SMTP username** and **password** (often the same as your IMAP credentials)

Use **port 993** with **SSL/TLS** for IMAP (recommended) or port 143 with STARTTLS. For SMTP, use **port 465** with **SSL/TLS** or port 587 with STARTTLS.

### App passwords

Many providers (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, and others) require an **App Password** instead of your regular account password when IMAP/SMTP access is enabled. Create one in your provider's security or account settings and use that password during setup.

## Enter servers yourself

If Toby can't find your servers, the setup sheet lets you enter them:

| Section | Field | Description |
| ------- | ----- | ----------- |
| Incoming mail (IMAP) | Server, Port | e.g. `imap.gmail.com`, `993` |
| Incoming mail (IMAP) | Use TLS | On for port 993 |
| Incoming mail (IMAP) | Username, Password | Your login and password or App Password |
| Outgoing mail (SMTP) | Server, Port | e.g. `smtp.gmail.com`, `465` or `587` |
| Outgoing mail (SMTP) | Use TLS | On for 465, off for STARTTLS on 587 |
| Outgoing mail (SMTP) | Username, Password | Often the same as IMAP |
| Sender | Name | What recipients see on messages Toby sends |

Toby checks the IMAP and SMTP connections before saving. Passwords are stored
in your Mac's Keychain.

## Read messages in Toby

Emails in [The Feed](../toby-app#read-an-email) open in Toby's email reader.
Toby fetches the message from your mailbox when you open it and leaves it unread
until you mark it read.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** next to **Mail** in **Settings → Sources**. Toby removes the
saved credentials from your Keychain; your mail at the provider is untouched.

## Example chat prompts

Switch to the built-in [**Mailman** persona](../personas#mailman) for inbox triage (Needs attention / Worth noting / Ignore, plus category labels).

- "Summarize my unread email from today and list anything that needs a reply."
- "Find threads about the Q2 launch and suggest archive or label actions."
- "Search my inbox for messages from alice@example.com from last week."
