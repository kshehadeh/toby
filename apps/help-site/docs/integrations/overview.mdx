# Sources overview

Sources are the apps Toby reads from and acts on. They are built into Toby: no
plugins to install, no servers to run. Once connected, Toby can search,
summarize, and take action when you ask in chat, and they feed **Home** and
[follow-ups](../followups).

## How to connect

- **Email and Slack:** **Settings → Sources → Connect…**. Passwords and tokens
  are stored in your Mac's Keychain.
- **Apple Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and macOS controls:** nothing to set up.
  macOS asks for permission the first time Toby needs it; review access in
  **Settings → Permissions**.

**Web search** and **weather** are built in and need no setup.

See [Connect your apps](../getting-started/configure-and-status) for a
walkthrough.
