<BrandTitle icon="/img/integrations/slack.png">Slack</BrandTitle>
Connect Toby to Slack to catch your mentions on **Home**, search messages, send
messages, and (optionally) let Toby answer Slack DMs and @mentions.

Toby connects through **your own Slack app**, so you control its scopes. You
need it once per workspace.

| You want… | Toby needs |
| --------- | ---------- |
| Mentions on Home, search, and sending from chat | **Sign in with Slack** (stores a user token), **or** a bot token |
| Toby replying in Slack | A **bot token** (`xoxb-…`) and an **app-level token** (`xapp-…`) |

## 1. Create your Slack app

The fastest way is Slack's app manifest, which sets the redirect URL, PKCE,
scopes, Socket Mode, and event subscriptions for you.

1. Go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps).
2. Click **Create New App → From an app manifest** and pick your workspace.
3. Paste [`slack-app-manifest.json`](/slack-app-manifest.json) and create the app.
4. Under **OAuth & Permissions**, click **Install to Workspace**.

The manifest registers `http://localhost:9878/callback` as the redirect URL,
enables PKCE, and requests these **user** scopes for sign-in: channel, group,
DM and group-DM read and history, `users:read`, `users:read.email`,
`chat:write`, and `search:read`.

<Callout type="note" title="Why user scopes?">

Slack only issues user tokens to desktop (localhost) sign-ins, so message
search and reading run as **you**. Replies run as the app's **bot**, which is
why they need separate tokens.

</Callout>

## 2. Connect in Toby

Open **Settings → Sources** and click **Connect…** next to **Slack**.

**Sign in with Slack (recommended)**

1. Paste your app's **Client ID** (from **Basic Information → App Credentials**).
   The client secret is optional.
2. Click **Continue in Browser** and approve the app.
3. Toby stores the tokens in your Mac's Keychain and shows the workspace name.

**Bot token**

Choose **Bot token** and paste the **Bot User OAuth Token** (`xoxb-…`) from
**OAuth & Permissions**. The bot posts as the app, and message search is not
available without sign-in.

**Disconnect** in **Settings → Sources** removes all Slack tokens from this Mac.

## 3. Let Toby reply in Slack (optional)

1. In your Slack app, open **Basic Information → App-Level Tokens**, create a
   token with the `connections:write` scope, and copy it (`xapp-…`).
2. In Toby's Slack **Connect…** sheet, under **Chat replies**, paste the
   **Bot token** (`xoxb-…`) and the **App-level token**.
3. Open **Settings → Chat Replies**, turn on **Reply to Slack messages**, and
   pick the persona Toby replies as.

Toby connects over Socket Mode while your Mac is awake and Toby is running.
See [Inbound chat](../configuration/inbound-chat).

### How Slack maps to Toby chats

| You talk in… | Toby treats it as… | How to continue |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --------------- |
| A channel or group **thread** (after you @mention the bot) | One chat for that thread | Keep replying in the thread |
| A **new** top-level @mention | A new thread and a new chat | Use that thread for follow-ups |
| A **DM** with the app | One chat for the whole DM | Keep messaging; no @ needed |

Top-level channel messages without an @mention are ignored. Deleting the chat
in Toby starts a fresh one the next time someone writes in that thread or DM.

## Troubleshooting

- **Sign-in fails with a redirect error:** the app must list
  `http://localhost:9878/callback` under **OAuth & Permissions → Redirect URLs**
  and have PKCE enabled.
- **"Port 9878 is in use":** close whatever is using that port and try again.
- **Replies don't start:** check that both tokens are set and that the app has
  Socket Mode enabled and the bot events from the manifest. The sidebar shows
  the reply status; details appear in **Help → Logs**.
