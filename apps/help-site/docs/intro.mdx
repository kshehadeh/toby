# Welcome to Toby

Toby is an AI assistant that lives on your Mac. It connects to the apps you
already use, such as your email, calendar, reminders, and to-do list, so you can
ask for help in plain English instead of switching between them.

Ask "What needs a reply today?" and Toby reads your inbox. Ask "Move my 3pm to
tomorrow" and it updates your calendar. Ask it to record a meeting, and you get
a transcript and a list of action items when the meeting ends.

![Toby summarizing the unread email that needs a reply](/img/toby-app-main.png)

## Who Toby is for

Toby is for anyone whose day is spread across too many apps. You don't need to
be technical to use it. If you can type a question, you can use Toby. It works
especially well if you:

- Get more email than you can read, and want to know what actually matters
- Juggle meetings and want notes and action items without typing them up
- Keep tasks in Todoist or Apple Reminders and want help deciding what to do next
- Do the same report, recap, or check-in every week and want it done for you

## What Toby can do

<FeatureHeading icon="bot" tone="ai" title="Answer questions about your day" />

Connect your email, calendar, tasks, Slack, Notion, or Jira, and Toby can read
and act on them for you. It can summarize, search, draft replies, create
events and reminders, and pull information from several apps into a single
answer. See [Integrations](./integrations/overview).

<FeatureHeading icon="mic" tone="mic" title="Record and summarize meetings" />

Toby records your microphone and the audio from other apps (like Zoom or
Teams), turns it into a transcript, and writes a summary with decisions and
action items. See [Recordings](./listen).

<FeatureHeading icon="schedule" tone="schedule" title="Do routine work on a schedule" />

Ask Toby to do something every morning, every Monday, or at the end of each
month, such as "summarize my unread email at 8am" or "draft my weekly status
update on Fridays." See [Schedules](./schedules) and [Flows](./flows).

<FeatureHeading icon="persona" tone="persona" title="Remember what matters to you" />

Tell Toby your preferences once ("I prefer meetings after 10am") and it
remembers them in future chats. You can also keep reference files in your
[Library](./library), group related work into [Projects](./projects), and
create [Personas](./personas) that change how Toby writes and what it
prioritizes.

<FeatureHeading icon="apple" tone="macos" title="Control your Mac" />

Turn Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth on and off, change the volume, check the battery, run
a Shortcut, or hide distracting windows, all by asking. See
[macOS](./integrations/macos).

<FeatureHeading icon="message" tone="chat" title="Chat from Slack" />

Mention Toby in Slack and it replies in the thread, using the same knowledge and
connected apps as on your Mac. See [Chat surfaces](./chat-surfaces/overview).

## How Toby works, in brief

- **It runs on your Mac.** Toby is a native macOS app. Your settings, chat
  history, recordings, and passwords are stored on your computer, and
  passwords are encrypted. See [Security](./security).
- **It uses an AI model you choose.** Toby sends your requests to an AI
  service such as Vercel AI Gateway, OpenRouter, or OpenAI, or to a model
  running on your own Mac with Ollama. You add one key during setup. See
  [Set up AI](./getting-started/setup-ai).
- **It only sees what you connect.** Toby can only reach the apps you connect
  in **Settings → Integrations**, and you can disconnect them at any time.
