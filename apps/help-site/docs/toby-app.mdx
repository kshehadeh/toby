# Toby app tour

This page shows you around the Toby app: what each area is for, where to find
things, and how to customize it. Each section links to a full guide.

## The main window

![The Toby main window: sidebar on the left, a chat in the middle, toolbar at the top](/img/toby-app-main.png)

- **Sidebar (left).** Switch between areas: **Home**, **Chats**, **Todos**,
  **Projects**, **Library**, **Connections**, **Recordings**, **Flows**, and
  **Skills** in one list.
- **Bottom of the sidebar.** The persona picker on the left chooses which
  [persona](./personas) new chats use. The colored dot on the right shows
  whether Toby's background service is healthy. Green means everything is
  working. Click the dot for details or to restart it.
- **Toolbar (top right).** **Record** (●) starts a meeting recording,
  **Settings** (gear) opens settings, and **Search** opens the command
  palette. A second group holds actions for the current screen, such as
  **New**, **Edit**, **Run now**, or **Delete**.
- **Lists.** Areas that hold many items (chats, projects, recordings, and so
  on) show a list in the middle column. Click an item to open it. Click empty
  space in the list to go back to the overview.

## Home

Home is your daily dashboard. It shows **The Feed**: a prioritized list of what
needs your attention across email, calendar, and tasks, each item with a short
reason and buttons to act on it. Under each title, a source line shows which
connection it came from and, when available, the sender, list, or project
(for example, **Apple Reminders · Errands**).

### Read an email

Click an email's title or **View email** to read the original message in Toby.
The reader shows the sender, recipients, date, attachments, and the message as
it was sent. Use the buttons at the top to draft a reply, archive, or mark the
message read; **⋯** has the rest, such as adding a todo or opening your webmail.
Acting on a message closes the reader and clears it from The Feed.

- **Opening an email doesn't mark it read.** Click **Mark read** when you're done.
- **Remote images are blocked** so senders can't tell you opened a message.
  Click **Load images** to show them for that message.
- Click an attachment to preview it with Quick Look, or the download icon to
  save a copy.

### Guide The Feed

Click **Guide The Feed** in the Home toolbar (or choose it from The Feed's
**⋯** menu) to tell The Feed what to include and what matters most, in plain
language. For example, "Put anything from my manager first" or "Leave out
GitHub notifications."

The sheet lists every instruction you've written, newest first. Use the switch
to turn one off without losing it, or the trash button to delete it. When you
close the sheet after a change, The Feed updates with your instructions. The Feed is always on and uses your default persona.

One-click buttons you create with [flows](./flows) appear in the **Actions**
sidebar on the right. Click the sidebar icon in the toolbar to hide or show it.
Flows can't add cards to Home.

### Customize the Actions sidebar

Click **Edit Home** (pencil icon) in the toolbar. It appears once you have at
least one Actions button. While editing you can:

- **Hide** a button. Hidden buttons move to a **Hidden actions** area below
  The Feed. Click **Show** to bring one back.
- **Color** a button with the palette button. **Use default** follows the
  flow's own color; **Neutral** removes color.

Click **Done** or press **Escape** when finished. Your choices are saved on
this Mac.

More options are under **Settings → General → Home**:

- **Hide onboarding checklist** hides the setup checklist. You can also click
  **Hide** next to the checklist's progress count.
- **Reset Home layout** shows hidden actions, clears action colors, and
  restores the Actions sidebar.

## Chats

Where you talk to Toby. Press **⌘N** for a new chat, or use the arrow next to
**+** to start one with a specific persona. See
[Your first chat](./getting-started/first-chat).

The **Working…** section starts collapsed. While Toby works, the step it is
running stays visible under the header. It shows what the tool was asked
(such as the search query or file path) and live progress, such as each query
being searched. That progress disappears when the step finishes and leaves a
one-line result, such as "8 results · reuters.com, europa.eu +5". Behind-the-scenes
steps, like checking which tools are available, appear only while they run.

Click the section to see every step and the selected tools, then click again to
collapse it. Your choice stays in place when work finishes or fails; completed
work shows **Worked for** with its duration. When the same tool runs several
times in a row, the step shows a count such as **×3**. Click its arrow to see
each call.

## Projects

A project keeps the chats, instructions, and files for one piece of work
together. See [Projects](./projects).

![A project's Details tab with its summary, folder, and files](/img/toby-app-projects.png)

## Library

Files you want Toby to remember and search, such as PDFs, notes, and images.
See [Library](./library).

## Connections

A map of the people, threads, and topics Toby knows about and how they link.
Drag to pan, zoom in or out, search for a name, or use the legend to show only
certain kinds of items. Select an item to see everything it connects to.

## Recordings

Meeting and call recordings with transcripts and AI summaries. See
[Recordings](./listen).

## Flows

**Flows** have manual, schedule, and event triggers. Their steps are
step-by-step recipes that can power one-click Home buttons. See
[Schedules](./schedules) and [Flows](./flows).

## Todos

Choose **Todos** (⌘7) to manage built-in todos, repeating tasks, checklists, and
reminders without connecting an account. See [Todos](./todos).

## Skills

Instructions that teach Toby how to do a specific task your way. See
[Skills](./skills).

## Memories

Facts and preferences Toby remembers about you. Memories open in their own
window: **View → Memories** (**⌘6**). See [Memories](./memories).

## Command palette

Press **⌘K** (or click **Search**) to jump to any chat, project, recording,
setting, or action by typing part of its name. If you type a sentence that
doesn't match anything, such as "summarize my day", choose **Start a chat
with…** and Toby opens a new chat with that request.

or **Clear** to disable it.

## Toby's Head

Choose **View → Show Toby's Head**, or **Show / Hide Toby's Head**
in Toby’s menu bar menu, to show or hide the floating head. Toby appears
as a portrait cutout without a window frame. Drag the portrait to move it; click
it to open **How can I help?** with a multiline question field.
You can also choose **View → Ask Toby's Head** or press **⌥⌘T** to open
the composer directly with the keyboard while Toby is active. The global
**⌃⌥Space** shortcut works from other apps, shows the head if hidden, opens its
chat bubble, and focuses the question field. Press it again to refocus the
current conversation. Configure it in **Settings → General**.
The bubble starts compact, with a small inset around the header and question
field, and expands when you send a question. Near the bottom
of the screen, it automatically slides upward to keep the whole conversation
on screen without moving Toby.

Use **Send** or **⌘Return** to ask Toby. Return inserts a new line. Replies
stream into the conversation, including Markdown and any choices Toby asks you
to make. Toby's Head shows the conversation without skill, tool, or preparation
details. Errors and interactive questions
still appear; full activity details remain available in the saved chat in Chats.
Toby's Head uses your configured default persona and AI
provider. Your first question creates a chat in **Chats**; follow-up questions
continue that chat. **Start over** begins a new chat and keeps the previous one
in your history. **Stop** cancels an active reply.

Closing the bubble or hiding Toby preserves the conversation and lets an active
reply finish. Reopen it to continue. If Toby cannot connect or create a chat, your
question stays in the input box so you can retry. The head's current
conversation lasts until you start over, switch Toby home directories, or quit
the app; saved chats remain available in **Chats**.
Toby’s eyes follow the cursor while idle and center when you open the
conversation. Cursor positions stay on your Mac and are not sent with messages.
Toby smiles subtly while the conversation is open. When you close the bubble,
he briefly frowns for about two seconds, then returns to his normal expression.
Reopening the conversation restores the smile.
While a question is processing, Toby raises one eyebrow. As the reply streams
in, his mouth moves subtly as though he is talking, pausing when text delivery
pauses. These expressions settle when processing finishes or Toby waits for
your choice.
Enable macOS **Reduce Motion** to keep the eyes still and switch mouth
expressions without animated transitions. The processing eyebrow stays static,
and talking motion is disabled.

Escape or clicking elsewhere closes the conversation while preserving its
draft. Right-click the portrait and choose **Hide Toby's Head** to hide
both surfaces. Toby's Head starts visible in the lower-right corner and remembers
both its position and whether you showed or hid it between app launches. The chat
bubble opens when you ask Toby; it does not reopen automatically at launch.

## Settings

Open **Settings** with the gear button or **⌘,**. Pick a section on the left.
Most sections have their own guide. See [Settings overview](./configuration/overview).

### General

These preferences apply to this Mac only.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| ------- | ------------ | ------- |
| **Home directory** | The folder where Toby keeps your data. Changing it points Toby at a different folder. It doesn't copy anything. | `~/.toby` |
| **Start at login** | Open Toby automatically when you log in | Off |
| **Show menu bar icon** | Quick access to chat, recording, and windows from the menu bar | On |
| **Global shortcuts** | System-wide shortcuts for the command palette, recording, and new chat | Not set |
| **Show tutorial button** | Show a button in the main toolbar to start the current section’s tutorial | On |
| **Theme** | System, Light, or Dark | System |
| **Accent color** | Color for buttons and highlights | Orange |

### Section tutorials

Choose the **tutorial button** (question mark) in the permanent main toolbar to
start or restart the tutorial for the section you are viewing. For example, in
**Flows** it starts the Flows tutorial; in **Home** it starts the Home tutorial.
Home, Chats, Projects, Library, Recordings, Flows, Skills, and Todos each have their own
tutorial. Replaying one section does not reset progress in the others.

The Home tutorial introduces the dashboard and workspaces. The Flows tutorial
invites you to inspect a built-in flow, explains **How it works**, and opens a new
flow draft. It explains triggers, sources, AI, and output destinations, scrolling
the editor to reveal the output area. The other sections currently provide a
short introduction.

Tutorials start when you request them. Choose **Next** to continue, or **Finish
tutorial** at the end. **Stop tour**, closing a tip, or switching sections ends
the current session. Use the toolbar button to start that section again.
Exploring the tutorial does not run a flow or save a draft; you can customize and
save the draft afterward or choose **Cancel**.

Use **Settings → General → Show tutorial button** to show or hide the toolbar
button. This setting controls the button’s visibility, and there is no separate
restart button in Settings. Progress is saved on this Mac; setup help and update
notices are separate from tutorials.

## Troubleshooting

- **Toby isn't responding.** Quit and reopen Toby. **Help → Logs** shows what
  background work (mail, Home, follow-ups, flows) last did.
- **Mail or Slack stopped working.** Open **Settings → Sources**, disconnect it,
  and connect again.
- **AI requests fail.** Check **Settings → Account**: you may need to sign in
  again or add credits.
- **macOS didn't ask for a permission, or you clicked Don't Allow.** Open
  **File → Permissions…** and click **Allow**, or **Open System Settings**
  if it was previously denied.
- **Something else.** Use **Help → Report an Issue…**.
